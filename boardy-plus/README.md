# Boardy+ session simulator

Open `index.html` in a browser. Choose a fictional family and advance its two transcripts one stage at a time. Both sides receive the same evidence. Download JSON to retain the complete scripted pair, selected controls and playhead.

Boardy+ is an original simulation UI in Devs 2.0. It does not contact Boardy or an LLM. All three example families are public fixtures, excluded from future held-out use. The advertised 120/240/120 mix and 20-point primary bar are design values from the draft protocol. Custody, complete held-out families, runtime pins and freeze remain governed by the benchmark repository. These examples are neither scored runs nor evidence of a Boardy advantage.

The switches expose direct answering, provenance labeling, criticism without revision, and revision with or without the adversarial critic. The configured experiment retains its named A–E arms. A full factorial study would require a separately specified amendment, a matched resource policy and independent scoring. The fixture outcomes include unsupported claims, appropriate abstention, unnecessary abstention and conflict handling. Exported records are marked simulated and must stay outside production scoring inputs.

The existing site build stages the simulator under `_site/boardy-plus/` for the Devs 2.0 Pages workflow.
