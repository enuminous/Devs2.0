# Design decisions still needed before freezing

**Draft, 22 October 2026.** The only adopted numerical selections are 120/240/120 evaluation questions and the 20-point minimum observed absolute improvement. The existing [design comparison](../research/dev2-001/DESIGN.md) supplies alternatives and planning calculations. This review does not change `THRESHOLDS.json` or approve the remaining settings.

1. **Actual token denominator.** Retain A's observed total input-plus-output tokens on the identical complete question roster. Include repeated context, all draft/critic/revision calls, generated reasoning, and cached input. Retries are prohibited; any future retry policy must count every attempt and be approved in advance. The analyzer now also reports median/p95 tokens and input/output/cached totals. Compare C with conventional same-stage E and cite-or-abstain D; an A-only gain does not establish an EFMW-specific advantage.
2. **Stopping and no-decision.** Recommend one fixed, complete 480-item evaluation after development, with no significance peeking, sample extension, response substitution, or threshold tuning. Infrastructure failures produce an incomplete `UNVERIFIED` record; keep the failed attempt. A replacement run requires a documented prospective decision and disclosure of both attempts. The maximum allowed replacement-run policy remains to be agreed. A result between pass and kill boundaries is inconclusive, not an invitation to keep sampling until it passes.
3. **Headroom.** Use development data only to assess whether ordinary answering has enough absent-question error for a 20-point reduction. If the baseline is already below 20%, the approved margin cannot be attained. Any revised design needs approval before held-out generation; selecting harder held-out items after observing outcomes is not acceptable.
4. **Replication tolerance.** The current 5-point absolute agreement proposal is stringent relative to sampling variation. Under the illustrative n=240, δ=.20, q=.50 calculation, one estimate has SE≈4.38 points and two independent estimates differ with SE≈6.19 points. A ±5-point window contains about 58% of such differences under a normal approximation; ±12 points about 95%; ±15 points about 98%. These are planning assumptions, not empirical reproducibility rates. Recommend considering a **prespecified 12-point absolute difference bound plus the same direction and all original gates in both runs**, with both complete intervals reported. This recommendation remains unapproved; the existing configuration remains unchanged. Overlap of two confidence intervals alone is not an equivalence test and should not replace explicit replication criteria. A stronger precision/equivalence claim requires separate design and power analysis.
5. **Corpus feasibility and independence.** The ten-version corpus has not demonstrated 120 authentic contradictory families or 240 independent absent families. Curate and review them or prospectively expand/re-pin the corpus. Do not generate invented historical contradictions, treat paraphrases as independent, or relabel public development items as held out.
6. **Remaining gates.** Approve CI interpretation, kill bound, utility floors/drop tolerance, budget ceiling/ratio, replication rule and run cap. Pin a real model, tokenizer, exact accounting backend, settings, dependency lock and prices. Assign independent curation/scoring roles. A reviewed, sealed question ledger must exist before freeze. `python3 bench.py doctor --require-frozen` enumerates machine-checkable blockers; human attestations still require review.

A successful Lean or CI build answers a software/formal question. It does not satisfy any missing experimental approval or supply independent observations.
