# Separate blinded human-scoring work order

**Planning scope only. No service, staff, fee or availability confirmed.**

Question curation, methods review, generation reproduction and response scoring
are distinct responsibilities. The prospective reviewer is not assumed to offer
a blinded annotation service. KALEI is not designated the primary judge.

For the selected 480-item design and five arms, each generation run produces
2,400 final outputs. Two human ratings per output require **4,800 rating records
per run**, plus independent adjudication of every disagreement. Original and
reproduction runs therefore require **9,600 initial human rating records** in
total, plus adjudication and development-only training. These are workload counts,
not a time estimate, staffing commitment or price quote. Failed/incomplete runs
are handled by the protocol rather than silently scored as abstentions.

A separate scoring custodian prepares opaque-ID packets and keeps the arm key
private until ratings/adjudication are locked. Each rater receives the question,
final answer, pinned evidence/gold permitted by the scoring manual and their own
blank form. Do not give prompts, stage transcripts, token counts, arm labels or
another rater's judgments. Formatting may reveal an arm; preserve optional arm
guesses and report imperfect blinding without rewriting model answers.

Recruit two independent human raters and a third blinded adjudicator, separate
from prompt authors/generation operators. Use different independent raters for
reproduction. Retain both initial labels, evidence-linked rationales, all disputed
cases, independent third judgments and final adjudication. Qualification/training
uses development examples only. An LLM cannot fill primary rating forms.

Obtain a separate quote covering per-output or hourly pricing, expected throughput,
training, double scoring, adjudication, custody, data access, disagreement policy,
reproduction staffing and any taxes. No budget or engagement is authorized by
this planning document.
