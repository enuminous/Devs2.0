## What remains necessary

For any new Tier C candidate, provide both authentic dated versions, exact incompatible
claims, the same referent and conditions, a neutral question, expected conflict-aware
answer, evidence-family relations, and an account of any explicit correction.
Independently review it and verify that retrieval presents both sides. Absence of a
claim in an older version, changed conditions, new evidence, and corrected citation
markers are not enough. Do not manufacture historical disagreement.

The approved target of 120 contradictory evaluation items/families is not currently
demonstrated feasible. Options for prospective review are an expanded authentic
corpus with fresh whole-corpus absence audits and a new manifest, or a documented
design amendment before freeze. This packet makes neither change and supplies no
independent approval. The bounded search is not a claim that no conflicts exist
elsewhere in the broader research portfolio or unpublished history.
